About Me

My interest in business is anchored in how things actually function behind the scenes. Long before an item arrives on a retail shelf or reaches a doorstep via an e-commerce platform, a complex choreography of sourcing, transportation, warehousing, and inventory balancing takes place.

I find myself drawn to the operational mechanics of business—asking why particular distribution channels work, how companies handle supply shocks, and how data tools like Excel and Power BI translate operational complexity into clear decisions.

Through my dual education journey at Kurukshetra University and IIM Bangalore, I am building both the quantitative grounding of commerce and the forward-looking mindset required for technology-driven business models.
